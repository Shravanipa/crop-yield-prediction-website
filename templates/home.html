Why AgriForecast

Planting decisions, backed by data — not guesswork

Most Indian farmers choose crops on intuition alone, with no access to data-driven yield estimates — leading to wrong crop choices, over-procurement of inputs, and poor insurance pricing for agricultural lenders.

🎯 The Problem

Without localized yield estimates, farmers over- or under-invest in seed, fertilizer and irrigation — and lenders can't price crop insurance fairly because they can't predict outcomes at the district level.

✅ Our Solution

  • A Random Forest regression model trained on state, district, season, crop, area sown and rainfall to predict expected yield per hectare.
  • A simple, mobile-friendly web tool — farmers or extension officers enter crop and location details and get a forecast instantly.
